ProCGroups.LocalWeight.MetrizabilityAndQuotients

9 Theorems | 1 Definition

This file develops local weight for quotients and open subgroups, relates countable local weight to descending open-normal chains, and characterizes metrizability of profinite groups. It also records consequences of topological finite generation.
