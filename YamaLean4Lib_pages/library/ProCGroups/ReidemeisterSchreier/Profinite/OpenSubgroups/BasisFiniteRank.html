ProCGroups.ReidemeisterSchreier.Profinite.OpenSubgroups.BasisFiniteRank

4 Theorems

Starting from a finite converging-set basis, this module constructs a basis of the open subgroup and proves the classical finite Schreier rank formula. The padding lemma works on so that its index lives in the ambient universe; its public generating-family wrapper is indexed by , while the bundled free basis used in the final rank argument retains the lifted carrier.
