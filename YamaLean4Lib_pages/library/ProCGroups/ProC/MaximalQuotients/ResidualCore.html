ProCGroups.ProC.MaximalQuotients.ResidualCore

2 Theorems | 1 Definition | 1 Structure

ProCQuotientKernel C G bundles a closed normal subgroup together with an open-normal quotient-in-\(C\) basis on its quotient. The residual core is their intersection; closedness and normality therefore follow directly from the stored kernel data, without reconstructing separation axioms from the basis property.

Definition ProCGroups.ProC.proCResidualCore 
noncomputable def proCResidualCore
    (C : FiniteGroupClass)
    (G : Type u) [Group G] [TopologicalSpace G] [IsTopologicalGroup G] : Subgroup G :=
  sInf (Set.range fun N : ProCQuotientKernel C G => N.toSubgroup)

The residual core \(R_C(G)\), defined as the intersection of all closed normal kernels whose quotient has an open-normal quotient-in-\(C\) basis.
