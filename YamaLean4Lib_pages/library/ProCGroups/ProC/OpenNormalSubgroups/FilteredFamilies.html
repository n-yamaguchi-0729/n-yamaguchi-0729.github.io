ProCGroups.ProC.OpenNormalSubgroups.FilteredFamilies

7 Theorems

This file proves compactness and intersection lemmas for directed families of closed and open subgroups. These results produce finite-intersection neighborhood bases and control images of intersections under continuous surjections.
