ProCGroups.ProC.Subgroups.Closed

7 Theorems

The pro-\(C\) basis passes to closed subgroups when \(C\) is subgroup-closed, to Hausdorff continuous quotients when \(C\) is a formation, and across a closed normal extension when \(C\) is extension-closed.
