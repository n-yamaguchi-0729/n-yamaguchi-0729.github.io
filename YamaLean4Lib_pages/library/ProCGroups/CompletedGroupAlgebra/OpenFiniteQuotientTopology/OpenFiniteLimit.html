ProCGroups.CompletedGroupAlgebra.OpenFiniteQuotientTopology.OpenFiniteLimit

3 sections | 3 files | 25 declarations

This aggregate exports the two-parameter inverse limit \(\varprojlim_{I,U}(R/I)[G/U]\), its opaque named carrier, canonical bundled quotient projections, inherited topological-ring structure, and the dense canonical map from \(R[G]\).

The carrier's compatible-family realization is available only through completedGroupAlgebraOpenFiniteQuotientCompatibleFamilyEquiv; ordinary consumers should use completedGroupAlgebraOpenFiniteQuotientLimitProjection and completedGroupAlgebraOpenFiniteQuotientLimit_ext.
