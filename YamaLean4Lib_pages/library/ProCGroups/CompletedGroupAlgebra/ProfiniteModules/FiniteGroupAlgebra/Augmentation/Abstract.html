ProCGroups.CompletedGroupAlgebra.ProfiniteModules.FiniteGroupAlgebra.Augmentation.Abstract

25 Theorems | 8 Definitions

This file defines augmentation of an abstract group algebra, its linear form and kernel ideal, and the resulting short exact sequences. It also proves that the differences \(g-1\) generate the augmentation ideal.
