This module translates simplicity of G ⧸ K into a dichotomy for normal subgroups above K and derives the intersection property needed for noncommutative simple quotients.

theorem normal_subgroup_eq_kernel_or_top_of_simple_quotient
    {G : Type u} [Group G] (K L : Subgroup G) [K.Normal] (hL : L.Normal)
    [IsSimpleGroup (G ⧸ K)] (hKL : K ≤ L) :
    L = K ∨ L = ⊤

If \(G/K\) is simple, every normal subgroup of \(G\) containing \(K\) is either \(K\) or all of \(G\). This is the correspondence theorem form of the simple-quotient dichotomy.

If subgroups above \(K\) satisfy the two-point dichotomy induced by a simple noncommutative quotient, then two normal subgroups whose products with \(K\) equal \(G\) retain that property after intersection.
