15# Continuous completed Fox calculus
16
17This aggregate imports the topology and continuity of completed Fox coordinates, their naturality
18and chain rules, free-source and universal constructions, topological-generation formulas,
19semidirect-kernel bases, automorphism formulas, and the resulting tail exactness theorems. The
20common algebraic `CrossedHom` interface is supplied by `FoxDifferential.Common`; finite-stage and
21completion-specific implementations are collected here.
