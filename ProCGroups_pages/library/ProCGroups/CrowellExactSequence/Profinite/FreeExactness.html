ProCGroups.CrowellExactSequence.Profinite.FreeExactness

6 Theorems

For a free pro-\(C\) source, this file combines continuous Magnus injectivity, the completed boundary calculation, and bifiltered finite-stage exactness. The resulting theorems assemble the presented and separated Crowell exact sequences under a surjective presentation map.
