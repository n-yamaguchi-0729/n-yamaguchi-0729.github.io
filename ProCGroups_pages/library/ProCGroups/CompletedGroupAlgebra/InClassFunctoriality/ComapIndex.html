ProCGroups.CompletedGroupAlgebra.InClassFunctoriality.ComapIndex

5 Theorems | 2 Definitions

An open-normal quotient index on the target pulls back along a continuous group homomorphism to an index on the source. This file defines that comap, the induced finite quotient map, and its monotonicity, compatibility, and surjectivity properties.
