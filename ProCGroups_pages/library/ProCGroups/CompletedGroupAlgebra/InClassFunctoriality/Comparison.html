ProCGroups.CompletedGroupAlgebra.InClassFunctoriality.Comparison

8 Theorems

This file computes the canonical maps between all-finite and in-class completed group algebras on group-like elements and their differences from one. It also records the corresponding formulas for functorial maps and restricted scalar actions.
