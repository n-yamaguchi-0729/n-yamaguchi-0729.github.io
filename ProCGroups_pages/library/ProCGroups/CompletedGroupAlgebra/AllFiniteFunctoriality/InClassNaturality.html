ProCGroups.CompletedGroupAlgebra.AllFiniteFunctoriality.InClassNaturality

9 Theorems

This module proves that the comparison between the all-finite and \(C\)-indexed completions is natural for continuous group homomorphisms, in ring-, algebra-, and equivalence-valued forms.
