ProCGroups.Topologies.ContinuousMonoidHom

8 Theorems | 3 Definitions

This file provides standard continuous homomorphisms involving subgroups and quotients, proves an open-mapping criterion for compact-to-Hausdorff surjections, and develops range restrictions. Kernels and ranges are shown closed under the expected separation hypotheses.
