ProCGroups.Generation.WordProductsAndClosure

10 Theorems

The word-product filtration is related to multiplication, inversion, and the abstract subgroup generated by a set. Under compactness hypotheses its finite levels are compact or closed, which supports separation of a finite level by an open normal subgroup and later finite-quotient generation arguments.
