ProCGroups.ReidemeisterSchreier.Profinite.OpenSubgroups.ExactRightSchreierGeneration

11 Theorems | 1 Instance

Using a transported right-coset section and the wreath-product embedding, this module identifies the concrete right-Schreier cocycles that topologically generate an open subgroup. It also proves the finite-quotient lifting statements used to recognize the resulting dense abstract free group as the required pro-\(C\) completion.
