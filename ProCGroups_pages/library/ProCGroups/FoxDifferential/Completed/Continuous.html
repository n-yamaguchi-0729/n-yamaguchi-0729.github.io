ProCGroups.FoxDifferential.Completed.Continuous

12 sections | 31 files | 679 declarations

This aggregate imports the topology and continuity of completed Fox coordinates, their naturality and chain rules, free-source and universal constructions, topological-generation formulas, semidirect-kernel bases, automorphism formulas, and the resulting tail exactness theorems. The common algebraic CrossedHom interface is supplied by FoxDifferential.Common; finite-stage and completion-specific implementations are collected here.
