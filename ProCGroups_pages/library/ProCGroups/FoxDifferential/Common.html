ProCGroups.FoxDifferential.Common

6 sections | 6 files | 177 declarations

This aggregate collects the algebraic interface shared by the discrete and completed theories: bundled crossed homomorphisms, their universal differential modules, Fox boundary identities, free-source differentials, finite-family linear maps, and Jacobian constructions. It does not choose a completion topology or a finite-stage coefficient system.
